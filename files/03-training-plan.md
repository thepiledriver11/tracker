# 12-Week Training Plan — 6 Days, 3 Aug to 25 Oct 2026

**Goal:** lean muscle gain.
**Structure:** 6 lifting days, stepper at the end of every session, 5 walks per week, one full rest day.

**The key change from 4 days:** you only have 4 distinct sessions, so Push and Pull each run twice — once heavy, once as a lighter volume day. Running both heavy twice a week would bury you inside a month. Lower stays at two sessions (Lower 1 and Lower 2) since those are already the most demanding.

---

## The Weekly Layout

| Day | Session | Walk |
|---|---|---|
| **Mon** | Push A (heavy) + stepper | ✅ 45 min |
| **Tue** | Pull A (heavy) + stepper | ✅ 45 min |
| **Wed** | Lower 1 + stepper | — |
| **Thu** | Push B (volume) + stepper | ✅ 45 min |
| **Fri** | Pull B (volume) + stepper | ✅ 45 min |
| **Sat** | Lower 2 + stepper | — |
| **Sun** | Rest | ✅ Long walk, 75–90 min |

Still no walks on lower days — legs get the stepper and nothing else. The four weekday walks sit on upper days; separate them from the lift by a few hours where you can (walk in the morning, lift after work, or vice versa).

---

## The Set Protocol

### Heavy days (Push A, Pull A, Lower 1, Lower 2)
Your trainer's format — 3 rounds, 2 exercises paired, weight drops each round.

| Round | Load | Reps | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Heaviest working weight | 8–10 | 1–2 reps in reserve |
| 2 | −15% | 10–12 | 1 rep in reserve |
| 3 | −15% again | 12–15 | To failure |

Rest: ~20 sec between the two exercises in a pair. 90–120 sec after each round.

### Volume days (Push B, Pull B)
Same pairing structure, different intent. Lighter, higher reps, shorter rest, stop short of failure.

| Round | Load | Reps | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | ~70% of your heavy-day weight | 12–15 | 3 reps in reserve |
| 2 | −10% | 15–18 | 2 reps in reserve |
| 3 | −10% again | 18–20 | 1 rep in reserve |

Rest: 60 sec between rounds. These should feel like a pump, not a fight. If you're grinding on a B day, you've gone too heavy.

**Progression rule:** only track and progress Round 1 on **heavy days**. B days follow whatever your A-day weights are doing. When you hit the top of the rep range on Round 1 for two heavy sessions running, add 2.5–5%.

## The Sessions

### Push A — heavy (Mon)
Warm-up: 5 min easy stepper, band pull-aparts, 2 ramp-up sets on DB press.

- **A1** Dumbbell press → **A2** Machine chest press — 3 rounds
- **B1** Cable flys → **B2** Machine shoulder press — 3 rounds
- **C1** Dumbbell lateral raises → **C2** Cable triceps pushdown — 3 rounds
- **D** Seated overhead DB extension — 3 descending sets

Stepper to finish.

### Pull A — heavy (Tue)
Warm-up: 5 min easy stepper, band pull-aparts, light lat pulldown.

- **A1** Lat pulldown → **A2** Cable bar biceps (cable between legs) — 3 rounds
- **B1** Machine row, undergrip → **B2** Cable rope straight-arm pulldown — 3 rounds
- **C1** Cable crossovers (rear-delt) → **C2** Prone incline bench DB curls — 3 rounds

Stepper to finish.

### Lower 1 (Wed)
Activation: banded abductions, 2 × 20.

- **A1** Hamstring machine → **A2** Bulgarian deadlift, straight leg — 3 rounds
- **B1** Single-leg Smith squat (rear foot on bench) → **B2** Leg extension — 3 rounds
- **C** Calf raise — 3 descending sets, 1 sec pause at the bottom

Stepper to finish (short).

### Push B — volume (Thu)
No heavy free-weight pressing. Machines and cables only, so your shoulders and elbows get a break.

- **A1** Machine chest press → **A2** Cable flys — 3 rounds
- **B1** Machine shoulder press → **B2** Dumbbell lateral raises — 3 rounds
- **C1** Cable triceps pushdown → **C2** Seated overhead DB extension — 2 rounds

Stepper to finish. Should take ~35 min.

### Pull B — volume (Fri)
- **A1** Lat pulldown (wide, lighter) → **A2** Cable rope straight-arm pulldown — 3 rounds
- **B1** Machine row, undergrip → **B2** Cable crossovers (rear-delt) — 3 rounds
- **C1** Cable bar biceps → **C2** Prone incline bench DB curls — 2 rounds

Stepper to finish. ~35 min.

### Lower 2 (Sat)
Activation: banded abductions, 2 × 20.

- **A1** Kettlebell lunges → **A2** RDLs — 3 rounds
- **B1** Hamstring machine → **B2** Leg extension — 3 rounds
- **C** Calf raises — 3 descending sets

Stepper to finish (short).

**Single-leg note:** on Bulgarians, single-leg Smith squats and KB lunges, "3 rounds" means 3 rounds *per leg*. Both legs before dropping the weight.

## Stepper Protocol

Six days of stepper adds up, so each dose is shorter than it would be on a 4-day split. Conversational pace — this is not a second workout.

| Block | Upper days | Lower days |
|---|---|---|
| **Weeks 1–5** | 8 min steady | 6 min steady |
| **Weeks 7–11** | 12 min (last 4 as 2 × 30s harder / 90s easy) | 8 min steady |
| **Deload weeks** | 6 min easy | 6 min easy |

---

## Walk Protocol

| Block | Weekday walks (×4) | Sunday long walk |
|---|---|---|
| **Weeks 1–5** | 45 min | 75 min |
| **Weeks 7–11** | 50 min | 90 min |
| **Deload weeks** | 30 min, easy | 60 min, easy |

Brisk but nasal-breathing pace. Hills fine on Sunday; keep the weekday walks flat and easy.

## The 12 Weeks

| Week | Starting | Block | Focus |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Mon 3 Aug | **Foundation** | Establish Round 1 loads on heavy days. Log everything. Deliberately go too light on B days. |
| 2 | Mon 10 Aug | Foundation | First progression pass on heavy days. |
| 3 | Mon 17 Aug | Foundation | Tighten heavy-day rest to 90 sec. |
| 4 | Mon 24 Aug | Foundation | Add the third round back to pair **C** on both B days. |
| 5 | Mon 31 Aug | Foundation | Peak of block — Round 3 to genuine failure on heavy days only. |
| 6 | Mon 7 Sep | **Deload** | Cut to 4 days: Push A, Pull A, Lower 1, Lower 2. 2 rounds, ~70% load. |
| 7 | Mon 14 Sep | **Overload** | Back to 6 days. Reset heavy loads ~2.5% above Week 5. |
| 8 | Mon 21 Sep | Overload | Add a double-drop to the final pair on heavy days (Round 3 → strip → strip). |
| 9 | Mon 28 Sep | Overload | Hold the double-drop. Progress Round 1. |
| 10 | Mon 5 Oct | Overload | Add a 4th round to pair **A** on Push A and Pull A. |
| 11 | Mon 12 Oct | Overload | Hardest week. B days stay easy — resist the urge. |
| 12 | Mon 19 Oct | **Deload / reassess** | 4 days, 2 rounds, 70%. Retest Round 1 weights against the Week 1 log. |

## Tracking

One line per exercise, heavy days only:

```
Lat pulldown — R1: 70kg × 9  (target 10 next time)
```

Round 1 weight × reps on heavy days is the only number that tells you whether the plan is working.

**Expect** upper-body Round 1 loads up roughly 10–15% by Week 12, lower body a bit less on single-leg work.

---

## Reading the Warning Signs

Six lifting days, five walks and daily stepper is a genuinely high workload. It works if you're recovering. Watch for:

- **Round 1 loads flat or falling for two weeks straight** — the plan is out-running your recovery.
- **Resting heart rate creeping up, or sleep getting worse.**
- **B days starting to feel like A days.**

If any of those show up, the fix is to **cut Push B or Pull B** and go to 5 days. Don't cut the walks or the food — those are the things making the 6 days possible.

## Supporting the Plan

- **Protein:** ~1.6–2.2 g per kg bodyweight daily.
- **Energy:** with this much training volume you'll need to eat deliberately to gain. A modest surplus (~300 kcal above maintenance) is the difference between this plan building muscle and it just making you tired.
- **Sleep:** 7–8 hours. At six sessions a week this is the binding constraint, not the programming.
- **Missed session?** Skip it, don't double up. Missing a B day costs you almost nothing.

---

*3 Aug – 25 Oct 2026. Adjust loads and durations to how you're actually recovering — the structure matters more than the specific numbers.*
